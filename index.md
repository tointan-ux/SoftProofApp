# SoftProof

Prepress soft proofing for professional plate inspection: Harlequin page buffers, 1-bit TIFF
separations, tiled pyramids for multi-gigapixel plates, preflight checks, and ICC colour
management through LittleCMS.

## Status

The viewer, the TIFF strip/pyramid engine and the preflight checks are the mature parts.
Colour management and the IDG press simulation are present but experimental; see the README
for what is production-ready.
